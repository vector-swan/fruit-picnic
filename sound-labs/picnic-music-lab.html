Picnic Music Lab

Background music styles for Fruit Picnic. Play the song to hear just the music, or Play with game sounds to have a pretend player swipe, merge and make new fruits on top. Open Change the song on any card to adjust its tempo, chords, instruments and parts while it plays. Everything is in the same key as your merges (C major pentatonic), so the merges land as part of the song. The tune is made up as it plays and changes a little every eight bars, so it never repeats exactly. Start with No music to hear the game as it is today.
