Privacy Policy

Fruit Picnic · Effective October 27, 2026

Fruit Picnic is a small puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your phone or in your browser so it remembers you: your best score, your sound, vibration and theme settings, whether you’ve seen the tutorial, and, if you use the leaderboard, your leaderboard name, a random player number, the groups you’re in, which boards you last chose to post to, and any players you chose to hide. Deleting the app (or clearing the website’s data in your browser) removes all of it.

The friends leaderboard (optional)

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to post a score, or to start or join a group. When you post, you tick which boards it goes to (Everyone and/or your groups), and the game sends:

  • the name you chose,
  • your best score and your highest tile,
  • a random player number the game made up on your device. It isn’t linked to your phone, your Apple Account or anything else about you, and it is never shown to anyone.

Each board keeps your best score. Nothing is posted unless you tap Post score. If you post to Everyone, your name and best score are shown to everyone who plays the game. If you only post to your groups, only the members of those groups see them. Please don’t use your full real name.

The leaderboard is stored with Supabase, the database service we use. Like any web service, Supabase receives your internet (IP) address when the game connects to it and may keep it briefly in technical logs. We don’t save it with your score.

Friend groups (optional)

You can start a leaderboard group or join one with a link or code. We store the group’s name and code, your leaderboard name, which player numbers are in it, and each member’s best score in that group (counting only games posted to the group since they joined). Anyone with the link or code can join and see the names and scores of the group’s members. When the last member leaves, the group is deleted.

Keeping names friendly

Names are checked against a list of words that aren’t allowed. If you tap Report name, we store that your player number reported that name. A name reported by two players shows as “Picnic Player” until we review it. Hide this player only hides them on your own device and isn’t sent anywhere.

Removing your score

Open the leaderboard (the trophy button), tap your own name and choose Remove my score. Your name, score, group memberships and reports are deleted right away. You can also contact us below and we’ll remove them for you.

The website

The website version is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the pages.

Children

Fruit Picnic is fine for all ages. The only thing a player ever enters is a leaderboard nickname, and posting is optional. Parents can ask us to remove a child’s score at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
